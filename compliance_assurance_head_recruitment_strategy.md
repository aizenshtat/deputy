## 1. Executive Summary

Following a compliance failure during a UK regulator check in late 2025, the company requires immediate recruitment of a **Head of Compliance Assurance** to establish quality assurance oversight for the 33-person Compliance department across 8 regulatory jurisdictions.

**Key Strategic Insight:** "Весь комплаенс про удовлетворение регуляторов. Они очевидно с этим не справляются." (All compliance is about satisfying regulators. They obviously can't handle this.) - Alexey Kirienko, December 11, 2025

**Urgency Level:** HIGH - This is a CEO-initiated role (not regulator-mandated) to proactively prevent future audit failures and transform compliance from reactive to outcome-based.

**Benchmark Model:** Revolut's Compliance Assurance department under Pierre Decote (Group Chief Risk & Compliance Officer), featuring 30 business auditors reporting directly to CEO for 40 directors.

**Timeline Target:** Begin sourcing and interviewing immediately per CEO directive: "Начнём искать-собеседовать и дальше будет видно кто приходит" (Start searching and interviewing, we'll see who comes).

## 2. Role Context and Strategic Rationale

### 2.1 Root Cause Analysis: UK Regulatory Failure

**Incident Overview:**
- During UK regulator check, compliance failure occurred
- None of 33 Compliance team members knew UK regulations required an additional custodian license for the business model
- Local Compliance Officer unable to properly demonstrate compliance to auditors

**Identified Systemic Gaps:**
1. **Knowledge Gap:** Compliance team unaware of critical licensing requirements
2. **Training Gap:** Local officers unprepared for regulatory audits
3. **QA Gap:** "У Compliance сейчас нет никакого QA, поэтому они при проверках часто садятся в лужу" (Compliance currently has no QA, so they often fail during audits)
4. **Preparedness Gap:** Team not trained or tested for regulator interactions

### 2.2 Strategic Solution: Compliance Assurance Function

**Core Philosophy:**
"На любой процесс должен быть quality assurance" (Every process should have quality assurance)
- In IT: Dedicated QA function
- In Compliance: Currently absent, needs to be established

**Role Purpose:**
"Наш внутренний аудитор будет тренировать их на такие проверки" (Our internal auditor will train them for such audits)

**CEO Initiative Context:**
- This is Alexey's own strategic initiative, NOT regulator-mandated
- Proactive measure to transform compliance effectiveness
- Recognition that current 33-person team lacks oversight mechanism

### 2.3 Revolut Benchmark Model

**Organizational Structure (January 2026):**
- **Leadership:** Pierre Decote as Group Chief Risk & Compliance Officer
- **Oversight Model:** 30 business auditors report directly to CEO (Storonsky)
- **Ratio:** One supervisor per director (40 directors total)
- **Philosophy:** Strong control and oversight culture

**Key Insights from Revolut Model:**
1. **Direct CEO Reporting:** Compliance assurance reports to highest level
2. **Embedded Oversight:** Not peer supervision (which "так себе работает" - works so-so)
3. **KPI-Driven:** Need "KPI по откапыванию" (KPIs for finding issues)
4. **Future Vision:** AI supervisors for every employee, not just directors
5. **Department Name:** "Compliance Assurance" (same as Revolut)

**2026 Revolut Priorities (Relevant to Our Needs):**
- Scaling teams in line with company growth (we have 33 people, 8 regulators)
- Embedding data-driven capabilities across 2LOD teams
- Supporting 1LOD with LLMs for alert investigations
- International coordination across local, regional, global teams

**Revolut Compliance Philosophy (from Singapore Head interview, Jan 5, 2026):**
- **Outcome-based compliance:** Not checkbox exercises
- **Flat, agile structure:** Empowers innovation while ensuring compliance
- **Monitoring & Assurance:** Effective oversight mechanisms
- **Cultural Integration:** Compliance as core capability, not reactive function

### 2.4 How This Role Fits Into Compliance Transformation

**Current State:**
- 33 Compliance professionals
- 8 regulatory jurisdictions
- No QA function or systematic audit preparation
- Reactive compliance approach
- Frequent audit failures

**Target State (with Compliance Assurance Head):**
- Proactive quality assurance across all compliance processes
- Systematic training and audit preparation
- Regulatory knowledge verification and testing
- Outcome-based compliance framework
- Measurable KPIs for compliance effectiveness
- Direct CEO oversight of compliance quality

**Transformation Pathway:**
1. **Phase 1 (Months 1-3):** Assess current compliance gaps, establish QA framework
2. **Phase 2 (Months 4-6):** Implement systematic audit preparation and training
3. **Phase 3 (Months 7-12):** Build data-driven monitoring, scale team if needed
4. **Phase 4 (Year 2+):** Continuous improvement, AI integration for oversight

## 6. Interview Process and Assessment

### 6.1 Screening Criteria (Resume/LinkedIn Review)

**Pass/Fail Criteria:**
- [ ] 10+ years compliance/audit experience in financial services
- [ ] Multi-jurisdictional regulatory experience (minimum 3 countries)
- [ ] Leadership experience managing teams or projects
- [ ] Evidence of audit preparation or regulatory examination success
- [ ] Fintech, banking, or asset management background

**Scoring Criteria (1-5 scale):**
1. **Regulatory Breadth:** How many jurisdictions/regulators? (FCA, MAS, etc.)
2. **Audit Experience:** Direct regulatory audit preparation examples?
3. **QA Background:** Quality assurance or compliance oversight role?
4. **Fintech Relevance:** Revolut/Wise/similar experience or traditional finance?
5. **Seniority:** Head/Director level or still Manager?
6. **Career Trajectory:** Consistent progression or lateral moves?

**Target Score:** 20+ out of 30 points to advance to phone screen

### 6.2 Interview Stages

**Stage 1: Phone Screen (30 minutes) - Recruiter/HR**
- **Objective:** Verify basic qualifications, assess interest and fit
- **Key Questions:**
  - Motivation for role and company
  - Compensation expectations
  - Notice period and availability
  - High-level career background
  - Understanding of compliance assurance vs. traditional compliance
- **Outcome:** Advance 50% of candidates (top 5-10 per recruiter touchpoint)

**Stage 2: Hiring Manager Interview (60 minutes) - Future CCO or Senior Compliance Leader**
- **Objective:** Deep technical assessment of compliance and audit expertise
- **Key Topics:**
  1. Regulatory audit preparation experience (specific examples)
  2. Multi-jurisdictional compliance challenges
  3. Quality assurance framework design
  4. Training and team development approach
  5. Licensing requirements knowledge (probe UK custodian license scenario)
  6. Revolut or fintech compliance model familiarity
- **Assessment:** Technical scorecard (see Section 6.3)
- **Outcome:** Advance top 30-40% to CEO interview

**Stage 3: CEO Interview (90 minutes) - Alexey Kirienko**
- **Objective:** Assess strategic fit, cultural alignment, executive presence
- **Key Focus Areas:**
  1. **Strategic Alignment:** Understanding of "satisfying regulators" philosophy
  2. **Problem Solving:** How would you approach our UK audit failure scenario?
  3. **Transformation Approach:** How do you build QA culture in 33-person team?
  4. **Stakeholder Management:** Experience reporting to CEO/Board
  5. **Revolut Model:** Thoughts on direct CEO reporting and oversight ratios
  6. **Vision:** What does success look like in 6/12/24 months?
- **Case Study (Recommended):** Present UK audit failure scenario, ask for 90-day plan
- **CEO Assessment Criteria:**
  - Can I trust this person to prevent next regulatory failure?
  - Will they command respect from 33-person compliance team?
  - Do they have the urgency and drive to move fast?
  - Are they systematic thinkers who build processes?
- **Outcome:** Select 2-3 finalists for final round

**Stage 4: Panel Interview (2 hours) - Cross-Functional Stakeholders**
- **Participants:**
  - Current Head of Compliance (if applicable) or Senior Compliance Leaders (2 people)
  - CFO or COO (business operations perspective)
  - Board Member or Audit Committee representative (if feasible)
- **Format:**
  - 30 min: Case study presentation (candidates prepare 90-day compliance assurance plan)
  - 45 min: Panel Q&A on case study and approach
  - 45 min: Cultural fit and working relationship assessment
- **Assessment Focus:**
  - Collaboration: Can they work with existing compliance team?
  - Communication: Can they explain complex compliance matters clearly?
  - Pragmatism: Do they balance rigor with business enablement?
  - Leadership: Do they inspire confidence?
- **Outcome:** Consensus on top 1-2 candidates

**Stage 5: Reference Checks (Thorough)**
- **Minimum 3 references:**
  1. Former manager (ideally CEO or CCO)
  2. Peer or direct report (team dynamics)
  3. Regulator or external auditor (if possible - shows regulatory relationships)
- **Key Questions:**
  - "Describe their regulatory audit preparation approach and results"
  - "How did they handle compliance failures or gaps?"
  - "What was their impact on compliance team capabilities?"
  - "Would you re-hire them? Why or why not?"
- **Red Flags:** Hesitation on re-hire question, concerns about urgency/execution

**Stage 6: Offer and Negotiation**
- Present competitive offer (see compensation section below)
- Emphasize strategic role, CEO reporting, transformation opportunity
- Close within 48-72 hours of reference checks
