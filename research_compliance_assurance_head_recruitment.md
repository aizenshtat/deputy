## Part 1: Revolut Compliance Assurance Model (January 2026)

### Overview
On January 5, 2026, CoinsPaid Media published an interview with Claudia Hui, Head of Compliance, Singapore at Revolut, discussing their compliance assurance approach and governance framework.

### Key Insights from Revolut's Model

#### 1. Outcome-Based Compliance Philosophy
- Singapore's MAS (Monetary Authority of Singapore) emphasizes sound governance, effective risk management, and demonstrable outcomes rather than purely procedural compliance
- Revolut applies robust global standards as a baseline and enhances them to incorporate MAS requirements
- Includes local governance structures, risk assessments, and control frameworks aligned with MAS guidelines

#### 2. Core Principles
- **Strong Governance:** Clear accountability structures
- **Skilled Professionals:** Investment in compliance talent
- **Monitoring & Assurance:** Effective oversight mechanisms
- **Cultural Integration:** Compliance embedded as a core capability, not reactive or purely advisory

#### 3. Organizational Structure

**Risk, Compliance & Audit Team Configuration:**
- Flat, agile structure designed to keep the company safe and ready to grow
- Empowers innovation while ensuring compliance across the business
- Second Line of Defence (2LOD) teams with data-driven capabilities

**Leadership:**
- Pierre Decote serves as Group Chief Risk & Compliance Officer
- Regional heads (e.g., Claudia Hui for Singapore)
- Quality Control function within 2LOD

**Team Expansion Priorities (2026):**
- Scaling compliance teams in line with company growth
- Embedding data-driven capabilities across 2LOD teams
- Supporting First Line of Defence (1LOD) in implementing LLMs to streamline alert investigations
- International coordination across local, regional, and global teams

#### 4. Key Functions Under Compliance Assurance

The structure includes:
- Regulatory affairs, reporting and engagement including licensing
- Policy & standards governance
- Compliance & regulatory risk management
- Safeguarding & custody
- Marketing & communications compliance
- Risk assessments
- Compliance monitoring & assurance
- Compliance audits
- Horizon scanning & regulatory intelligence
- Training & awareness
- Whistleblowing and complaints

#### 5. Regional Hiring Focus (2026)

For GCC region expansion, Revolut is hiring:
- Regulatory Compliance Assurance Manager
- Business Compliance Manager
- Fincrime Risk Manager positions
- Regulatory Reporting Manager
- Tax Manager

### Essential Qualifications

**Experience Requirements:**
- Minimum 10 years of proven experience in a broad compliance role

- Experience with global expansion efforts

**Knowledge Areas:**
- Deep understanding of regulatory landscape (increasingly complex and fragmented in 2025-2026)
- AML/KYC expertise (top priority in fintech risk management)
- Data privacy & cybersecurity compliance
- Operational resilience (DORA compliance from 2025)
- AI governance and embedded finance compliance
- Cross-border regulatory frameworks

### Core Responsibilities

**Strategic Leadership:**
- Serve as internal and external ambassador on all matters of compliance
- Ambitious and visionary leadership
- Ensure team has clear top-down performance objectives linked to company-wide goals
- Leadership commitment with unwavering dedication and adequate resource allocation

**Operational Oversight:**
- Comprehensive, regular reporting to Board and governance committees
- Status reporting on compliance risks, regulatory changes, and remedial actions
- Build scalable compliance program with clear governance
- Implement automated controls and measurable KPIs

**Team Management:**
- Right balance of compliance knowledge, risk awareness, and technical expertise
- Prioritize hires based on exposure and fill highest-risk gaps first
- Strong training programs and awareness initiatives

### Reporting Structure

**Typical Reporting Lines:**
- Reports to: CEO or Chief Risk Officer
- Direct Reports: Quality Assurance Specialists, Compliance Managers, Regional Compliance Heads
- Board Interaction: Regular reporting to Board and relevant governance committees

### Key Competencies for 2025-2026

**Regulatory Focus Areas:**
1. **AML/KYC:** Identity verification, risk scoring, transaction monitoring
2. **Data Privacy:** GDPR, data breach prevention, security controls (top enforcement focus)
3. **Operational Resilience:** DORA compliance, IT risk management, incident response, third-party oversight
4. **Emerging Technologies:** AI governance, LLM integration for compliance processes

**Strategic Approach:**
- Focus on operational maturity, not just documentation
- Regulators expect controls that work in practice
- Build compliance as core capability alongside product development from early stages

## Part 3: Industry Context & Trends (2025-2026)

### Regulatory Environment

**Complexity:**
- Fintech regulation in 2025 is more complex, fragmented, and actively enforced than previous years
- Focus on outcome-based compliance rather than checkbox exercises
- Increased scrutiny on AI tools, salary transparency, and data privacy

**Key Regulatory Developments:**
- DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act) applies from 2025
- Enhanced AML/KYC requirements
- Stricter data protection enforcement
- AI governance frameworks emerging

### Team Structure Best Practices

**Three Lines of Defense Model:**

1. **First Line (1LOD):** Departmental leads across HR, engineering, sales ensuring local processes align with policies
2. **Second Line (2LOD):** Compliance and risk management teams providing oversight, monitoring, and assurance
3. **Third Line:** Internal audit assessing effectiveness of risk controls through independent reviews

**Cross-Functional Approach:**
- GRC teams work across governance, risk management, and compliance
- Ensure strong operational controls
- Comply with external regulations, laws, and requirements

### Hiring & Recruitment Trends

**Strategic Priorities:**
- Customized recruitment strategies for compliance roles
- Targeted approaches to quickly identify right candidates
- Proven ability to align compliance strategy with business objectives
- Advise senior leadership on complex matters
- Investment in skilled compliance professionals

**Cultural Considerations:**
- Foster culture where compliance and risk management are integral to business strategy
- Build compliance as core capability, not reactive function
- Make compliance top priority alongside product development
