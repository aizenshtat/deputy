## Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Resistance to Coordination
**Risk:** AI initiative leads may resist centralized coordination as "bureaucracy"
**Mitigation:**
- Frame as "support" not "control"
- Quick wins - demonstrate value (shared tools, cross-learning)
- CEO endorsement for AI Coordination Forum

### Risk 2: Resource Conflicts
**Risk:** Multiple initiatives competing for same AI talent, budget, infrastructure
**Mitigation:**
- Transparent prioritization framework
- Shared services model (AI CoE provides common capabilities)
- Advocate for increased AI budget if needed

### Risk 3: Strategic Misalignment
**Risk:** Tactical AI initiatives diverge from strategic vision (#ai-endgame)
**Mitigation:**
- Ensure #ai-endgame participants advise AI Coordination Forum
- Regular strategy refresh (quarterly)

### Risk 4: Compliance & Risk Blind Spots
**Risk:** AI initiatives may create regulatory, privacy, or ethical issues
**Mitigation:**
- Include Compliance Assurance Head (once hired) in AI governance
- Establish AI risk assessment framework
- Legal review of customer-facing AI features

## Appendix C: Assessment Framework for Channel Reviews

When reviewing #ai-endgame, #ai_code_generation, #sales-ai, use this framework:

### 1. Participation Analysis
- [ ] List all participants (name, role, frequency of posting)
- [ ] Identify the "core" group (regular contributors)
- [ ] Identify lurkers vs. active discussants
- [ ] Note executive involvement (CEO, C-level)

### 2. Activity Patterns
- [ ] Messages per week (trend over time)
- [ ] Peak activity periods (when are important discussions happening?)
- [ ] Thread vs. one-off messages
- [ ] Reactions and engagement levels

### 3. Content Themes
- [ ] Strategy vs. execution discussions
- [ ] Technical vs. business focus
- [ ] Decision-making moments (agreements, approvals)
- [ ] Blockers and escalations
- [ ] Wins and celebrations

### 4. Artifacts Created
- [ ] Canvas documents (roadmaps, strategies)
- [ ] Lists (task tracking, priorities)
- [ ] Shared documents (Google Docs, Confluence)
- [ ] Code repositories or demos shared

### 5. External Connections
- [ ] Mentions of other channels or initiatives
- [ ] Vendor discussions (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.)
- [ ] Customer/user feedback references
- [ ] Competitive intelligence

### 6. Cultural Indicators
- [ ] Enthusiasm level (emojis, celebratory messages)
- [ ] Skepticism or resistance
- [ ] Collaboration vs. siloed discussion
- [ ] Innovation vs. risk aversion
